How the maths works

Every class you skip adds 1 to the total but not to attended. Every class you attend adds 1 to both.

Formula

Classes you can skip

(100 × attended − 75 × total) ÷ 75, rounded down

Fill in your numbers above to see your exact calculation.
Formula

Classes you must attend

(75 × total − 100 × attended) ÷ (100 − 75), rounded up

Fill in your numbers above to see your exact calculation.

Loop or formula?

A loop would test one class at a time and need checks. The formula gets the answer in 1 step, however many classes you have.

Percentages here are rounded down, so 74.96% shows as 74.9%, never 75%.
